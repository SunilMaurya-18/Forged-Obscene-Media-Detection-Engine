# Brag Plan: Forged Media Detection

## What is this app?
A trust-and-safety platform that detects deepfakes and explicit imagery, routes uncertain cases to humans, races legal takedown deadlines, and seals every decision with a signed evidence trail — without ever storing the image.

## The angle
**SEOPulse-style product film:** real UI screens in a browser frame, simulated interaction, Kokoro narration, upbeat bed ducked under voice. Follow one clear journey: check → route to human → deadline queue → sealed proof. Not a trailer of title cards.

## Hook (first 6 seconds)
Brand canvas. Three typed lines: "Deepfakes take minutes." / "The law gives hours." / "We never keep the image."

## Key moments
- Real landing hero in a browser chrome
- Real Analyze screen with case-sample uploaded
- Real moderation queue with deadline chips
- Real Verify / seal moment → VALID / 0 stored claim
- Wordmark + CTA outro

## Outro / punchline
**Forged Media Detection. Act before the legal deadline.**

## User flow worth showing
Landing → Analyze check → Queue against the clock → Verify / sealed decision

## Tone
- Preset: `app-store`
- Creative direction: SEOPulse terminal-precise product film — dark brand canvas framing real product screens
- Interpretation: feature-forward, one idea per scene, clean slides, steady UI SFX, narrator leads

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 56 seconds (under 60s; narration sets pace)

## Visual identity
- Background: `#0f1114`
- Accent: `#d97757`
- Text: `#e3e6ea` / muted `#9aa1a9`
- Display: Source Serif 4; Body: Manrope; Mono: JetBrains Mono
- Strongest visual: real landing hero + queue cards with deadline chips

## Share copy (draft)
Deepfakes take minutes. The law gives hours. We never keep the image — only the proof. Forged Media Detection.

- SFX: key ticks, clicks, card drops, logo bell
